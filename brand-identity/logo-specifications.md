# Logo Specifications & Visual Asset Guide

## Logo Mark Structure
The **Coop Works - Consulting** emblem consists of three key visual elements:
1. **Outer Executive Shield & Gold Dash Border**: Represents biosecurity protection, enterprise governance, and structural operational integrity.
2. **Central Geometric Wing & Pillar Vector**: Symbolizes poultry growth, FCR trajectory improvement, and tech-driven farm scaling.
3. **Emerald & Amber Telemetry Nodes**: Represents real-time IoT logger telemetry, GPS geo-tagged mobile field audits, and data precision from **Poultry Resource Planner**.

## Typography Specifications
- **Brand Logotype**: `COOP WORKS` (Font: `Cinzel`, Weight: 800, Letter Spacing: -0.02em)
- **Subheading**: `CONSULTING` (Font: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, Weight: 700, Letter Spacing: 0.18em, Color: `#B45309`)
