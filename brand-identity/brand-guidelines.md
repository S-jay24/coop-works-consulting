# Coop Works - Consulting | Brand Identity & Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence & Vision
**Coop Works - Consulting** is an enterprise poultry consulting and technology firm headquartered in Karnataka, India. We specialize in contract broiler integration advisory, turnkey farm operational management, technical FCR audits, and our proprietary **Poultry Resource Planner** software suite.

- **Primary Identity**: `COOP WORKS`
- **Official Subtitle**: `CONSULTING`
- **Tagline**: *Technology-Driven Poultry Operations & Strategic Farm Advisory*

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Executive Palette
- **Deep Navy 900 (`#0A192F`)**: Dominant header, text, and structural container background.
- **Royal Blue (`#1E40AF`)**: Interactive buttons, primary links, and active telemetry indicators.
- **Executive Amber Gold (`#D97706`)**: Stat counters, badge borders, and accent highlights.
- **Emerald Performance (`#059669`)**: FCR efficiency metrics, positive ROI indicators, biosecurity status.

### Light Theme Background Surfaces
- **Page Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F8FAFC`)**: Light slate background for section contrast.
- **Card Container (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white elevation with subtle slate borders (`#E2E8F0`) and soft elevation shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).

---

## 3. Typography System
- **Brand Title / Monogram**: `Cinzel` (Serif elegance and heritage authority)
- **Executive Headings**: `Playfair Display` (Clean editorial prestige)
- **Body Text & UI Data**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (Precise, high-density corporate readability)

---

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines
- The official emblem combines an outer executive shield with a geometric poultry wing and tech pillar vector.
- Always retain `COOP WORKS` in `Cinzel` uppercase, paired with `CONSULTING` in tracking uppercase.
- Do not distort or alter the primary aspect ratio.
