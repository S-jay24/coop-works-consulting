# Coop Works Consulting — Brand Asset Kit

## Master Logo: The Crowned Apex

This kit contains high-definition vector (SVG) and raster (PNG) assets for the **Crowned Apex** brand identity.

---

### Brand Color Palette
- **Espresso Charcoal**: `#1C1917` (RGB: 28, 25, 23) — Used for the 'C' structural curve and primary typography.
- **Warm Golden Amber**: `#C4701B` (RGB: 196, 112, 27) — Used for the 'W' ribbon, chicken comb, wattle, beak, and sub-labels.
- **Eggshell Cream Background**: `#F7F1E3` (RGB: 247, 241, 227) — Brand primary surface background.
- **Inverted Golden Amber**: `#E8A246` (RGB: 232, 162, 70) — For dark theme & footer applications.

---

### Brand Typography
- **Primary Wordmark**: *Playfair Display* (Weight: 700 Bold / 900 Black)
- **Sub-label / Category**: *Inter* (Weight: 600 / 700, Spacing: `0.3em`, Uppercase)
- **Body Copy**: *DM Sans*
